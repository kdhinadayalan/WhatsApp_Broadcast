### MCA Curriculum and Subjects (Semester-wise)
The MCA program is a 2-year (4-semester) autonomous curriculum:

• *Semester 1*:
  - Advanced Data Structures and Algorithms
  - Object-Oriented Software Engineering (Java & Design Patterns)
  - Relational Database Management Systems (RDBMS & SQL)
  - Python Programming & Data Analysis
  - Data Structures & DB Lab

• *Semester 2*:
  - Full Stack Web Development (React & Node.js)
  - Advanced Operating Systems & Cloud Computing
  - Machine Learning Fundamentals
  - Mobile Application Development (Flutter/Android)
  - Full Stack Development Lab

• *Semester 3*:
  - Artificial Intelligence & Deep Learning
  - Cyber Security & Cryptography
  - Big Data Analytics & NoSQL
  - DevOps & Microservices Architecture
  - AI & Cloud Capstone Lab

• *Semester 4*:
  - 6-Month Full-Time Industry Internship & Project Dissertation

## Category: Admissions & Eligibility

### Documents Required for Admission
Students seeking admission must submit original and photocopies of:
1. *10th (SSLC) Marksheet*
2. *12th (HSC) Marksheet*
3. *Undergraduate Degree Marksheets & Provisional Certificate* (for PG / MCA applicants)
4. *Transfer Certificate (TC)* and Conduct Certificate from the last attended institution
5. *Community Certificate* (BC / MBC / SC / ST candidates)
6. *Aadhaar Card* copy
7. *Passport Size Photos* (5 copies)
8. *Migration Certificate* (for other university/state students)
9. *TANCET Score Card* (for Government quota MCA admissions)
Tags: documents required for admission, admission documents, certificates needed, tc, eligibility

### MCA Admission Eligibility Criteria
• *Eligibility*: Passed BCA / Bachelor Degree in Computer Science Engineering or equivalent degree OR Passed B.Sc. / B.Com / B.A. with Mathematics at 10+2 level or at Graduation level.
• *Minimum Marks*: At least 50% marks (45% in case of candidates belonging to reserved category) in the qualifying degree.
• *Admission Modes*: Merit Quota through TANCET counselling or Management Quota.

### College & Examination Fee Structure
• *MCA Tuition Fee*: ₹28,000 per semester
• *Semester Examination Fee*: *₹1,500* per semester (Theory: ₹150/paper, Practical: ₹250/paper, Marksheet: ₹150)

• *College Bus / Transport Fee*: ₹6,000 to ₹12,000 per year (depending on boarding route)
• *Hostel Fee*: ₹35,000 per semester (inclusive of boarding and south Indian mess meals)
• *Payment Methods*: Can be paid online via the College ERP student portal or at the Central Bank of India campus branch.

## Category: Rules & Regulations

### College Rules, Attendance, and Code of Conduct
• *Attendance*: Minimum *75% attendance* is strictly mandatory to appear for autonomous end semester examinations.
• *Dress Code*: Formal attire required. Laboratory coats mandatory during chemistry/physics labs. College ID card must be worn around the neck at all times inside campus.
• *Mobile Phones*: Mobile phone usage inside lecture classrooms and examination halls is strictly prohibited.
• *Ragging*: Anti-Ragging regulations are strictly enforced with zero tolerance.
